Approvals belong to the ingredient: approve “leave off the coleslaw” once and it applies to every dish with coleslaw, including dishes added later. Only approve a change the kitchen can always make. A dish shows as “Suitable with changes” only if every ingredient that brings the guest’s allergen is approved — with a large warning that they must tell their server. If Procure Wizard later changes an ingredient’s allergens, its approval is suspended automatically until someone re-approves it.
